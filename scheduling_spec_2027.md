## 2. Core Coverage & Quota Rules

1. **Daily Coverage:** Every calendar day from January 1, 2027 through December 31, 2027 must have exactly one member assigned to on-call duty.
2. **Weekday Eligibility (Mon–Thu):** Only **Full-Time (FT)** members may be assigned to Monday–Thursday shifts. **Alternates (ALT)** are strictly prohibited from working Monday–Thursday shifts, **EXCEPT** when an Alternate works a weekend that has an attached Monday holiday (see Section 5).
3. **Weekend Quotas (52 Weekends Total in 2027):**
   * **H1 (Jan 1 – Jun 30, 2027):** CHESTER, ZEIDERS, and REED are each assigned **exactly 3 weekends** (9 Alternate weekends total in H1). Full-Time members cover the remaining 17 weekends.
   * **H2 (Jul 1 – Dec 31, 2027):** CHESTER, ZEIDERS, and REED are each assigned **exactly 3 weekends** (9 Alternate weekends total in H2). Full-Time members cover the remaining 17 weekends.
   * **Annual Totals:** 18 total weekends covered by Alternates (6 per Alternate); 34 total weekends covered by Full-Time members (~11–12 weekends per full-year FT member, adjusted for KEMERER's Phase 1 weekends).
4. **No Consecutive Weekends:** No member (FT or ALT) may be scheduled to work two consecutive weekends in a row.
5. **Equity / Workload Balancing:** Total weekend shifts, total weekday shifts, and attached holiday shifts must be distributed as evenly as possible among the active Full-Time members.

## 3. Schedule Phases (Weekday & Weekend Structure)

### Phase 1: January 1, 2027 – February 28, 2027 (4 FT Members Active)
* **Standard Weekend Shift:** Spans **Friday, Saturday, and Sunday** (3 days), covered by 1 FT or 1 ALT member.
  * *Holiday Exception:* On attached Monday holidays (Jan 18 MLK Day and Feb 15 Presidents' Day), the member working that weekend (FT or ALT) also covers **Monday**.
* **Baseline Weekday Assignments (Mon–Thu):**
  * **Monday:** POLCHA *(unless covered by the weekend worker due to a Monday Holiday).*
  * **Tuesday:** KEMERER
  * **Wednesday:** WOLFE *(Must be swapped with another eligible FT member if WOLFE worked the prior weekend; see Section 4).*
  * **Thursday:** TEREK *(Must be swapped with another eligible FT member if TEREK worked the prior weekend; see Section 4).*

### Phase 2: March 1, 2027 – December 31, 2027 (3 FT Members Active)
* **Weekend Shift Definition:**
  * **When a Full-Time (FT) member works the weekend:** The shift spans **Friday, Saturday, Sunday, and Monday** (4 days).
  * **When an Alternate (ALT) member works the weekend:**
    * *Non-Holiday Monday:* The ALT member works **Friday, Saturday, and Sunday** (3 days). **Monday** is covered by one of the 3 FT members (POLCHA, TEREK, or WOLFE) on a strict equal rotation. *(Note: This results in that FT member working two weekday shifts during that week).*
    * *Attached Holiday Monday:* If Monday is an official holiday (Memorial Day May 31, Labor Day Sep 6, Columbus Day Oct 11), the ALT member working the weekend **also covers Monday** (4 days total: Fri–Mon).
* **Weekday Rotation (Tue–Thu):** **Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday** shifts are rotated evenly among the 3 active FT members (POLCHA, TEREK, WOLFE), strictly subject to the Post-Weekend Rest Rule in Section 4.

## 4. Post-Weekend Compensatory Rest Rule (Effective All Year)

When a **Full-Time (FT)** member works a weekend (Weekend $W$), they are scheduled on Regular Days Off (RDO) for 4 days the following week (Thursday through Sunday).
* **Hard Constraint:** The FT member who worked Weekend $W$ **CANNOT** be scheduled for on-call duty on the **Wednesday, Thursday, or Friday** of the week immediately following Weekend $W$.
* **Phase 1 Impact:** If WOLFE works Weekend $W$, WOLFE cannot work Wednesday of Week $W+1$. If TEREK works Weekend $W$, TEREK cannot work Thursday of Week $W+1$. Swap their weekday on-call shifts with an eligible FT member.
* **Phase 2 Impact:** With only 3 active FT members (POLCHA, TEREK, WOLFE), the FT member who worked Weekend $W$ (Fri–Mon) may **only** be scheduled on **Tuesday** during Week $W+1$ (if needed), leaving the other 2 FT members to split **Wednesday and Thursday** of Week $W+1$.

## 7. Mandatory Solver Rules for Blackout Weeks (Preventing Deadlocks)

Because Phase 2 only has 3 Full-Time members, whenever one FT member takes a full week off, only **2 FT members** remain available to cover Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. If one of those 2 remaining FT members worked the prior weekend, the Post-Weekend Rest Rule blocks them from working Wednesday and Thursday—leaving only **1 FT member** to cover both Wednesday and Thursday.

To prevent this mathematical impossibility, the scheduler **MUST** apply these rules:
1. **Weekend of May 7–9, 2027 (Prior to WOLFE's May 10–14 Leave):** Assign an **Alternate (ALT)** to the weekend of May 7–9. This ensures both **POLCHA** and **TEREK** are fully rested and eligible to split the weekday shifts during May 10–13 while WOLFE is off.
2. **Weekend of Jun 25–27, 2027 (Start of TEREK's Jun 26–Jul 3 Leave):** Assign an **Alternate (ALT)** to the weekend of Jun 25–27. This ensures both **POLCHA** and **WOLFE** are fully rested and eligible to split the weekday shifts during Jun 28–Jul 1 while TEREK is off.
3. **Weekend of Feb 26–28, 2027 (Both POLCHA and WOLFE Off):** With both POLCHA (off Feb 27–28) and WOLFE (off Feb 22–26) unavailable, this weekend **must** be assigned to either **TEREK**, **KEMERER** (if eligible before transfer), or an **Alternate (ALT)**.
